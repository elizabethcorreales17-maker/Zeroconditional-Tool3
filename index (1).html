What is the Zero Conditional?

We use the Zero Conditional for facts, rules, and results that are generally or always true.

🧩 StructureIf + present simple, present simple.
If you heat ice, it melts.
🏫 Classroom exampleWe can use it to talk about school habits and rules.
If students practice, they improve.

Remember: both parts use the present simple. You can put the if-clause first or second.
